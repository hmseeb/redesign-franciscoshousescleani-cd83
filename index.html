FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Call (267) 993-9465 — we answer 24/7.

How much does house cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the space and the service you need, so we quote every job individually. Estimates are free, and the price you get is a flat written price before we start — it doesn’t move on the day, and we won’t upsell you into a deep clean your home doesn’t need. New customers also get 10% off their first clean.

What’s the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean is the dependable maintenance visit — the regular room-by-room checklist, done properly, to keep the place in good shape. A deep clean is the top-to-bottom reset: the build-up a maintenance clean doesn’t reach. If you’re not sure which one your home needs, tell us about it and we’ll recommend the right one.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

That’s entirely your call — plenty of customers are home, and plenty arrange access ahead of time and come back to a finished house. Just let us know how you’d like us to get in when you book, along with any instructions, and the crew will follow them.

Which areas do you serve?

We’re based in Philadelphia and cover roughly a 30-mile radius across Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware — 15 locations in all, including Abington, West Chester, King of Prussia, Norristown, Bensalem, Upper Darby, Media, Doylestown, Bryn Mawr, Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Voorhees, Wilmington and Claymont.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Call at 6am or 11pm and you reach a real person — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Same-week availability is normal for us, and we schedule evenings and weekends at no premium.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes — our crews arrive with the supplies and equipment needed to work through the full checklist for your service. If you’d rather we use specific products of your own, tell us when you book and we’ll follow your instructions.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. You can book, ask questions and give instructions in English or Spanish, by call or text, and know they’ll be understood exactly as you meant them. Hablamos español.

What if I’m not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and redo it at no cost. No forms, no argument, no invoice.

Can I get a discount?

There are three ways to save: 10% off your first clean as a new customer, loyalty pricing on recurring weekly, bi-weekly and monthly plans, and a referral bonus — refer a friend and you both save on your next cleaning.
